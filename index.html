Insta360 X5 to PLY Workflow

Multiple paths from 360 capture to shareable 3D point clouds

Path A: Cloud (Easiest)

1

Capture 360 Video

Record 1-2 minutes of 360 video with your Insta360 X5. Walk slowly around the scene.

2

Upload to Splatica

Upload raw .insv files to splatica.com (official Insta360 partner). Processes in cloud.

3

Download PLY

Download the .PLY file (point cloud, Gaussian Splat, or mesh). Ready to view here!

Path B: Open Source (Full Control)

1

Transfer Files

Connect X5 via USB-C. Copy .insv files from DCIM/Camera01/ to your PC.

2

Stitch & Extract Frames

Use insta360-stitch (Python) or Insta360 Studio to get equirectangular frames.

3

Run SfM + 3DGS

Process with EasyGaussianSplatting or COLMAP for point cloud reconstruction.

4

Export & Share PLY

Export .ply file. Upload here or share via SuperSplat for online viewing.

Path C: PC-Based (LichtFeld Studio)

1

Export 360 Video

Export YouTube-compatible 360 video from Insta360 app.

2

360Gaussian Processing

Import into 360Gaussian v1.4.4. Extract frames at 1fps. Disable AutoMasker if needed.

3

LichtFeld Studio

Run 3D alignment in LichtFeld Studio v0.5.3. Train on GPU (RTX 4070+ recommended).

4

Export PLY

Export final .ply for web viewing and mobile sharing.
